        Score methodology

        Each active property receives up to 100 points: Revenue level 25 (relative to the portfolio), Trend vs. prior year 20 (−30% = 0, +10% = full; new units neutral 10), Occupancy vs. local market 15, Quality 20 (Booking score and Elev8 guest rating 2026), open bookings 10 and operational problems 10 (deduction per low rating ≤ 3/5). The formula can be viewed under “Sources” next to the score.

        Costs: Cleaning per booking and laundry per guest and booking (guests from Elev8; where 0 is recorded – mainly older imported bookings – the configured guest count applies) can be set above. Commission depends on the channel of each booking: Direct/website 0%, Booking.com and Airbnb at the rate measured in Elev8 (OTA commission ÷ gross revenue, August + September 2026 – before that, commissions are not or only partly recorded in Elev8), other OTAs at the adjustable default. Elev8 does not yet provide commission per booking, hence channel rates. Default values: CHF 90 cleaning, CHF 12 laundry per guest, 15% for other OTAs, 2 guests – changes apply to your own view; please ask Claude for new default values.

        Classification: Continue & expand · Continue – optimise (incl. long-stay and mandate models) · On probation with target until 31.03.2027 · Review exit / terminate. The recommendation is a professional assessment across score, trend and causes – not just the score.

        Important: Rent, cleaning and utility costs are not recorded in any connected system. The recommendation is based on revenue and quality data; before giving notice, please check the rent per unit in the contribution margin calculator.

        Sources

        Elev8 Suite (live): all reservations with tag “Swiss” (Switzerland) and “indonesia” (Bali), arrival Oct 2024 – Dec 2027. Revenue and nights are allocated to the period per night of stay; bookings and guests count by arrival date; open bookings = not yet completed (current and future). USD bookings at 0.80, IDR bookings (Bali) converted to CHF at the configured rate (default 21'000 IDR per CHF). Guest names are not loaded. Guest ratings 2026 and calendar notes are a snapshot from 15.10.2026.

        MyDataValue: Booking review score and categories (as of Oct. 2026).

        PriceLabs: Account review 10/2025–09/2026 (data effectively from May 2026), RevPAR vs. market, notes on seasonal profiles and base prices.

        AirROI: market KPIs for entire apartments (Schaffhausen, Neuhausen, Basel, Dietikon as proxy for Greater Zurich, Olten as proxy for Mittelland, Thun as proxy for Steffisburg), RevPAR trend Schaffhausen 24 months.

        HostBuddy: action items from guest communication (access, cleaning, maintenance, enquiries). The sentiment search was not reachable at the time of creation.

        Management mandates: Hasenberg Suite (ML) and Suites Hasenberg (BB) – Elevate receives 20% of net rental income (guest payment − OTA commission); cleaning and laundry are passed on to the owner 1:1; no rent and no furniture rental. “Result” here is the fee for Elevate.

        Costs per apartment are maintained in the tab of the same name and saved in the dashboard (visible to everyone). Total fixed costs = net rent + utilities + furniture + internet/TV + electricity + parking + software/lock + other + (deposit insurance + contents/liability) ÷ 12. Result after all costs = revenue − commission − cleaning − laundry − fixed costs × 12. Without recorded rent, no result after all costs is shown.

On an exit, furniture is returned without follow-up costs; after the contract ends it belongs to Elevate; properties without a contract are self-furnished. Empty values: cleaning = default above, rent = not deducted.
